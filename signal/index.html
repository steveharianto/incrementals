SIGNAL

You are decoding a transmission that never ends.
START HERE
Photons
The sky always emits. They accumulate on their own, forever.
Dish
Spend photons on one and it collects more. That is the whole first move.
Nothing else
There is no clicking to do. Leave it open and glance over.
THE ARRAY
Six tiers
Each tier builds the one below it, down to Dishes, which make photons.
Milestones
Every 10 purchases of a tier doubles its output. The thin line under a row tracks progress to the next.
Buying
Click a row for one. Shift-click for as many as you can afford.
LATER, IN THIS ORDER
Collapse
At 1e9 photons you may reset the array for Coherence, which multiplies all production. Resetting is how you grow.
Coherence
Holding it boosts production. Spending it buys upgrades that last until you retune.
Retune
Trade all coherence for Insight and one Band, chosen from three. Bands are kept forever and stack.
Tree
Insight buys automation. Every node is permanent and can be switched off. This is where the game starts playing itself.
Depth
The last node. Collapses convert more with every order of magnitude of insight, without ceiling.
GOOD TO KNOW
Saving
Automatic, in this browser. The SAVE tab exports and imports.
Offline
Time away still counts, at reduced efficiency until you upgrade it.
This panel
Reopen any time with the ? beside the header.
